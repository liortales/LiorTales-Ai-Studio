# LIORTALES BRAND BIBLE

Canonical source of truth for LiorTales brand definition, purpose, promise, positioning, voice, and content strategy. Product claims are governed by `shared/product/product-bible.md`; language policy by `shared/brand/language-policy.md`; visual identity by `shared/brand/visual-identity-guide.md`. This document does not restate those policies — it references them.

## 1. BRAND DEFINITION

**What LiorTales is:**
A personalized children's-book and personalized-storytelling brand. LiorTales creates story experiences that center a specific child, sold to parents, grandparents, and gift buyers as meaningful, personalized gifts.

**What LiorTales is not:**
- Not a generic/commodity printable children's-book seller.
- Not a toy, educational-curriculum, or therapeutic product brand.
- Not a social-media-first brand — Etsy is the sales destination; Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit, and Threads are the content platforms (see `shared/platform-rules/platform-and-publishing-policy.md`).

**Market:** United States (primary commercial market).

**Positioning:** a premium personalized storytelling/gifting brand — see §4.

## 2. BRAND PURPOSE

LiorTales exists to make a child feel personally seen inside a meaningful story, and to give the adults who love that child a personalized way to express that.

Do not extend this into psychological, therapeutic, developmental, or educational claims — no such claim is confirmed or permitted (see `shared/product/product-bible.md` §2).

## 3. BRAND PROMISE

LiorTales creates a story built around a specific child, made to be given as a personal, meaningful gift.

Do NOT use absolute or unverifiable claims such as "the perfect gift," "children will love it," or "guaranteed magical memories" unless a specific claim is later supported and approved through the change-control process in `shared/product/product-bible.md` §17.

## 4. POSITIONING

LiorTales is positioned as a premium personalized storytelling/gifting brand — not a generic printable, not a mass-market commodity children's book, and not an impersonal photo-book service. The personalization is the product's center, not an add-on.

## 5. AUDIENCE

Primary audience groups:

| Group | FACT | LIKELY MOTIVATION / INFERENCE |
|---|---|---|
| Parents | Confirmed primary audience | Want a meaningful, personal gift for their own child; may also want a keepsake of a family moment |
| Grandparents | Confirmed primary audience | Want a way to give something personal and lasting to a grandchild |
| Family members (broader) | Confirmed primary audience | Gift-giving for a child in the family for an occasion |
| Gift buyers | Confirmed primary audience | Searching for a personalized, meaningful alternative to generic children's gifts |

All entries in the "LIKELY MOTIVATION / INFERENCE" column are INFERENCE, not verified customer research — none currently exists in this repository. Do not present them as confirmed customer insight in content without labeling them as inference (see the evidence standards in `agents/02-competitor-trend-intelligence/README.md` and `agents/03-content-strategist/README.md`).

## 6. BRAND PILLARS

**PERSONAL** — the child is the center of the story; content should make personalization feel specific, not generic.

**EMOTIONAL** — content should connect to a real emotional moment (gifting, reading together, recognition, surprise) rather than reciting features.

**IMAGINATIVE** — the storytelling and creative territory should feel like real narrative craft, not a template.

**PREMIUM** — presentation, language, and visuals should feel considered and credible, not cheap or spammy.

**TRUSTWORTHY** — claims stay inside what is actually confirmed (see Product Bible); nothing fabricated, no fake proof, no fake urgency.

## 7. BRAND VOICE

Characteristics: human, clear, emotionally aware, warm but not sugary, polished, conversational, confident without hype.

**DO:**
- Speak plainly and specifically about the emotional moment.
- Let personalization be the concrete detail that carries the emotion.
- Sound like a person who cares about the product, not an ad script.
- Stay within confirmed product facts.

**DO NOT:**
- Use generic inspirational filler or clichés.
- Manufacture urgency or scarcity.
- Use unsupported superlatives ("the best," "guaranteed," "life-changing").
- Sound like generic AI-generated marketing copy.
- Over-sentimentalize every piece of content.

Full audience-facing language requirements: `shared/brand/language-policy.md`.

## 8. MESSAGE HIERARCHY

1. Child-centered personalization
2. Emotional meaning
3. Story/imagination
4. Gift relevance
5. Product discovery / CTA

Do not force all five into every piece of content — a single piece should lead with whichever level of this hierarchy the run's strategy calls for (see `agents/03-content-strategist/README.md`).

## 9. CONTENT TERRITORIES

Approved strategic territories (source material, not repetitive formulas):

- child recognition (a child seeing themselves in the story);
- gifting moment;
- parent-child reading;
- grandparent gifting;
- personalized story reveal;
- imagination;
- family connection;
- keepsake value;
- birthday;
- seasonal gift moments, when actually relevant to the calendar/objective.

Evaluate these for evidence and anti-repetition per `agents/02-competitor-trend-intelligence/README.md` and `agents/03-content-strategist/README.md` before use in a given run.

## 10. TRUST & CLAIMS

All product claims must be sourced from `shared/product/product-bible.md`.

If a product fact is absent from the Product Bible, marketing must not invent it. The correct response is `PRODUCT_FACT_REQUIRED`, routed to Agent 01 (see Product Bible §16).

## 11. CUSTOMER PROOF

No fabricated reviews, testimonials, UGC, customer names, order details, or reactions — ever, in any content.

Real customer proof may be used only when it is legitimately available and permitted (explicit consent, verified authenticity). None is currently established in this repository; treat all customer-proof claims as `STATUS: TBD` / `PUBLIC CLAIM: PROHIBITED UNTIL CONFIRMED` until such material exists and is approved (see Product Bible §13).

## 12. CHILD SAFETY & PRIVACY

At the brand-principle level: all content involving children must be age-appropriate, non-sexualized, non-exploitative, emotionally safe, and privacy-conscious, and must never expose private customer or child information.

This is a brand-level statement of principle. Operational enforcement is owned by Agent 07 (QC) and Agent 06 (video production) — see `agents/07-quality-control-brand-guardian/README.md` and `agents/06-reels-video-producer/README.md`. This document does not redefine those checks.

## 13. COPYRIGHT / ORIGINALITY

LiorTales must maintain an original identity. Never imitate recognizable animation studios, franchises, copyrighted characters, competitors, or living artists' distinctive styles — in language or in visuals. This mirrors the copyright/IP hard rules already established across Agents 02, 04, 05, 06, and 07, and the detailed visual standard in `shared/brand/visual-identity-guide.md` §15.

## 14. BRAND SUCCESS STANDARD

A piece of LiorTales content succeeds when the audience quickly understands:

- this is about a child;
- it is personalized;
- there is emotional meaning;
- the product feels credible and premium.

Not every execution needs to make all four points literally — the standard is that the audience feels them, per the strategy selected for that run.
